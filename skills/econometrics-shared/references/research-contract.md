# Empirical Research Contract

Create this contract before recommending a model or judging a result. Mark unavailable fields as `NOT PROVIDED`; do not infer them from convention.

## Contract fields

1. **Question**: State the answerable economic question in one sentence.
2. **Unit and population**: Identify the observational unit, target population, geography, and period.
3. **Treatment or exposure**: Define assignment, timing, intensity, and possible anticipation.
4. **Outcome**: Define construction, timing, scale, and measurement risk.
5. **Estimand**: Specify ATE, ATT, LATE, intention-to-treat effect, elasticity, dynamic path, descriptive parameter, forecast target, or another explicit target.
6. **Assignment and comparison**: Explain why treated and comparison observations reveal the estimand.
7. **Identifying assumptions**: List each necessary assumption separately.
8. **Observable implications**: Map each assumption to diagnostics or falsification exercises without calling diagnostics proof.
9. **Estimator**: State the estimator, controls, fixed effects, weights, sample, and functional form.
10. **Inference**: State the dependence structure, clustering level, uncertainty interval, and multiple-testing plan.
11. **Threats**: Prioritize selection, simultaneity, omitted variables, measurement error, spillovers, attrition, interference, specification search, and external validity as relevant.
12. **Claim boundary**: State what can and cannot be concluded if all reported evidence holds.

## Decision rule

Do not choose a final estimator when the estimand, assignment process, or unit of analysis is unclear. Ask only for missing facts that would change the design judgment; otherwise provide a bounded audit.
