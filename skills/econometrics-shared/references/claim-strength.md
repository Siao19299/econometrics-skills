# Claim Strength Ladder

Match prose to the strongest level supported by the design and evidence.

1. **Documented**: The supplied source or output directly reports the fact.
2. **Descriptive**: Variables co-move or groups differ in the observed sample.
3. **Adjusted association**: The relationship persists conditional on a stated model and controls.
4. **Design-based causal effect**: A clearly defined estimand is identified under explicit, defensible assumptions.
5. **Mechanism evidence**: Additional evidence distinguishes a proposed channel from plausible alternatives.
6. **Generalized claim**: Evidence supports transport beyond the studied units, place, and period.

Never move upward because a coefficient is statistically significant, multiple specifications have similar signs, or the prose would sound more compelling. Treat mediation regressions, heterogeneity patterns, and timing consistency as suggestive unless the design identifies the mechanism. Distinguish zero evidence from evidence of a substantively small effect using interval width and the smallest effect of interest.
