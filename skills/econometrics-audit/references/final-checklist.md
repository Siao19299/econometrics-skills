# Audit Final Checklist

- State the reviewed material and missing material.
- Define the estimand and unit of analysis.
- State every identifying assumption separately.
- Distinguish assumptions from diagnostics.
- Connect clustering and weights to the design.
- Check economic magnitude and uncertainty, not p-values alone.
- Separate pre-specified tests from exploratory robustness.
- Calibrate causal, mechanism, and external-validity language.
- Attach a supplied-text or supplied-output pointer to every substantive finding.
- Avoid requiring arbitrary robustness rituals with no identified threat.
- State when a method lies outside MVP coverage.
