# Regression Discontinuity Gate

Check:

- the running variable, cutoff, assignment rule, fuzzy versus sharp design, and local estimand;
- continuity of potential outcomes or local randomization assumptions;
- precise manipulation, sorting, heaping, and institutional exceptions around the cutoff;
- bandwidth selection, polynomial order, kernel, bias correction, and robust uncertainty;
- graphical evidence using transparent bins and local fits without relying on the graph alone;
- continuity of predetermined covariates and density diagnostics as falsification evidence, not proof;
- donut specifications, alternative bandwidths, placebo cutoffs, and sensitivity to influential observations;
- treatment receipt and first-stage discontinuity for fuzzy RDD;
- external-validity limits outside the cutoff neighborhood.

Discourage high-order global polynomials and causal claims away from the cutoff unless a separate design supports extrapolation.
