# Instrumental Variables Gate

Define the instrument, endogenous treatment, outcome, population, and target parameter before assessing validity.

Check:

- **Relevance**: report first-stage coefficient, uncertainty, and weak-instrument diagnostics appropriate to the specification.
- **Independence**: explain why the instrument is as-good-as-random conditional on the stated design.
- **Exclusion**: enumerate plausible direct paths from instrument to outcome and supporting institutional evidence.
- **Monotonicity or homogeneity**: state the assumption used to interpret the estimand.
- **First-stage population**: describe compliers and whether LATE language is used where appropriate.
- **Inference**: account for generated treatment predictions, clustering, multiple instruments, and weak identification.
- **Specification**: explain controls, fixed effects, sample restrictions, and instrument construction.

Do not treat a large first-stage statistic as evidence for exclusion. Overidentification tests do not prove that all instruments are valid. Avoid calling IV an ATE unless stronger assumptions justify it.
