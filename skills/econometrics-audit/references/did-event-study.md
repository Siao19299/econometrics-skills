# Difference-in-Differences and Event-Study Gate

Check:

- treatment timing, absorbing versus reversible treatment, anticipation, and never-treated or not-yet-treated comparison groups;
- the exact ATT or dynamic estimand and the population weights implicit in the estimator;
- whether parallel trends is credible in untreated potential outcomes, supported by institutional context and outcome-specific evidence;
- whether treatment timing is staggered and effects are heterogeneous, making conventional two-way fixed-effects event studies potentially misleading;
- cohort composition, support at each event time, and changes in sample composition;
- pre-period coefficients and joint uncertainty without treating non-rejection as proof of parallel trends;
- spillovers, concurrent policies, endogenous timing, Ashenfelter-type dips, and differential measurement changes;
- clustering at the treatment-assignment level and small-cluster concerns;
- sensitivity to comparison group, window, functional form, outcome definition, and anticipation period.

Require the estimator and comparison logic to match the estimand. Distinguish diagnostic pre-trends from the identifying assumption itself.
