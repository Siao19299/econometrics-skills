# Empirical Economics Review Gates

## Contribution

- Is the economic question precise and consequential?
- Is the contribution conceptual, empirical, methodological, measurement-based, or institutional?
- Is the literature position supported by verifiable comparisons?

## Design and identification

- Is the estimand explicit and economically interpretable?
- Does the comparison identify it under stated assumptions?
- Are institutional details consistent with those assumptions?
- Do diagnostics address specific threats without being presented as proof?

## Data and measurement

- Are data provenance, sample construction, attrition, missingness, merges, and exclusions transparent?
- Do treatment and outcome measures correspond to the theory and timing?
- Could measurement error, reporting behavior, or selection create the result?

## Estimation and inference

- Do specification, weights, fixed effects, and controls match the estimand?
- Does uncertainty match assignment and dependence?
- Are magnitude, precision, multiple testing, and heterogeneity interpreted correctly?

## Robustness and interpretation

- Are credible alternatives distinguished from the preferred interpretation?
- Are falsification tests tied to assumptions?
- Are causal, mechanism, welfare, and external-validity claims calibrated?

## Reproducibility and integrity

- Are data and code access described?
- Can tables, figures, and numbers be mapped to programs?
- Are restricted data and legal access handled transparently?
